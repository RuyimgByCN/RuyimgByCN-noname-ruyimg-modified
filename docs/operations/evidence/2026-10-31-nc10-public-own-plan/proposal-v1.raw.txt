If that intersection is empty, no deterministic public-only policy can meet the current promise without an explicitly changed public/authority contract.
- A usable public certificate must bind lifecycle/actor/revision/choice/selection and guarantee formal execution to its claimed boundary, including the actual consumer and every intervening scheduled old park/callback, implicit arithmetic/effect and fuel failure. It must be sound over the admitted restore domain, independent of secret-state routing; initial producer uniqueness is insufficient. This is a necessary invariant, not a proposed taint framework, second engine or registry.
- Existing Own schema contains no such guarantee. First seek a bounded proof for the exact selected action and public class; if unavailable, record unsupported. Publishing a new authority commitment or altering admission/resume/atomicity would be a separately reviewed contract/schema decision, not an inferred Source27 permission. Do not tighten restore or publish private continuations silently.
