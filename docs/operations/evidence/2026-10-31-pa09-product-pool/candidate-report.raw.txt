- [candidate_pool.rs:49](/tmp/pa09-candidate-f82ecf3c/worktree/crates/engine-native/src/ai/candidate_pool.rs:49) admits consecutive root indices on the owner only after cancellation, the one shared start/deadline, and the global node budget checks. It never schedules later unadmitted roots, never divides the node budget per worker, and never starts a job-local clock. At most `pool.workers()` accepted jobs are outstanding; a queue-rejected job computes once on the owner, so accepted plus local active work is also bounded by that count. Each root still consumes a node even if submit rejects it.
- Accepted jobs own their only result sender. The owner joins the oldest admitted result before admitting beyond the bound, drains all accepted jobs before reduction/failure, then reduces in original index order with the original strict `score > best` tie rule and fallback. A lost accepted job disconnects its receiver; no owner-held sender can mask that loss. Unexpected panic/channel loss fails visibly after draining, with no retry or `plan_all` missing-result recomputation. Caught trial-submit panics remain `sim_faults`, and their diagnostic pairs enter the existing bounded fault log in root order.
- [session.rs:630](/tmp/pa09-candidate-f82ecf3c/worktree/crates/engine-native-wasm/src/session.rs:630) lazily owns a native-only pool, with workers `min(available_parallelism,4)` and capacity equal to workers. All constructors initialize it empty; restores retain it; session replacement/drop closes and joins it through existing pool lifecycle. Finished/open-choice paths retain their behavior. WASM retains the original nonthreaded `plan_action` route. No worker owns a pool or waits on owner/IPC locks.
- The one approved observational addition is [PlannerPool::submitted_jobs](/tmp/pa09-candidate-f82ecf3c/worktree/crates/engine-native/src/ai_pool.rs:81), an `AtomicUsize` with Relaxed operations incremented only after successful `try_submit`. [NativeSession::planner_jobs_submitted](/tmp/pa09-candidate-f82ecf3c/worktree/crates/engine-native-wasm/src/session.rs:390) exposes it only as a native Rust getter.
