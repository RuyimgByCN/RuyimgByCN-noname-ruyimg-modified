Minimal persisted shape and ordering (proposed fields/states below are additions, not existing APIs):
- Keep tag85, owner/selected-def/window and the existing Slot frame; derive root from that exact window rather than duplicate it. Add one effect-specific stage enum: Ready, AwaitResponse, AwaitLoss, AwaitJudge, AwaitCardAfterCard, Finished. Payloads hold only the branch data needed below: captured original source/recipient, chooser/selected target, option/count, opaque candidate binding, judge progress, and awaited child EventId(s).
- Ready is authenticated at start from the queue/window and moves to its next stage before returning. Every card move/cost/log/child emission changes stage and records its child binding in the same atomic command segment; AwaitLoss distinguishes holder payment from source payment. The stage is the exactly-once payment/effect receipt, not a turn flag. Never take a cost twice after restore.
- Emit using existing `Ctx::emit` and `Flow::Yield`; record `res.next_event_id` before emission because `emit` returns `()`. Await each loss/transfer/draw/card subtree before asking or applying the next effect. Do not open a choice in the step that emits a child: WaitChoice changes resume depth to the current stack length (`resolution.rs:522`) and can otherwise bind the wrong consumer.
- Choices bind to the admitted stage, exact actor, open ID, legal current zones/counts and exact candidate set. Resume validates those local facts and receipt/parent/depth relationships; it does not rerun provider ownership or admission filters after admission. Preserve captured causal source even though `run` can clear a now-dead root source (`resolution.rs:471`); apply each downstream operation's own liveness rule.
- Reuse `take_owned_card(player,card)` (`machine.rs:438`) for HE removal/revocation/`note_cards_lost`/CardsLost; `discard_from_hand` handles only H. For a selected HE discard batch, validate the whole batch before mutation, remove each once, send to discard, emit ordinary CardsDiscarded/log as required by its normal consumer, and await all loss children. Do not silently convert equipment cost into a hand cost.
- Qinglong transfer uses those ordinary loss semantics plus CardsMoved/transfer receipt; `give_hand_cards(from,to,&[CardId])` (`machine.rs:625`) supplies the existing event pattern but cannot directly take E. Preserve source event ordering with Yield between loss and follow-up transfer consumers; no new generic transfer framework.
